# Benchmarks

Generated by `build/pairing_bench` (`bench/bench.cpp`) from fixed seeds; see `bench/books.hpp` for the generators. Requirements and counts are deterministic; times depend on the machine.

**Mixed runs (2026-11-26).** "Solve time by size" and "Pre-trade check" were regenerated on the owner's Mac (M3 Max, Apple clang) with `pairing_bench timing` and `pairing_bench orders`, after the generators were made portable (the same seed now gives the same book on every platform) and after the Phase 6 solver changes. The other tables are still from the Linux run (g++ 13.3) on the earlier, platform-dependent books, and have not been regenerated.

## Savings against greedy

Each family's books are solved three ways under the default rule set: every position margined on its own, the greedy baseline (`greedy` in `src/greedy.cpp`), and the solver. Books in these families have up to about 40 series, so the solver runs exact (`rel_gap = 0`). Savings are relative to the all-standalone requirement; the excess is greedy's requirement above the solver's, relative to greedy's.

## Batch throughput

Accounts that hold the same series in different sizes share the model's matrix and costs; only bounds differ (spec §3.2), so one account's root basis can start the next. Each batch is 200 accounts on one chain: the same series and signs, sizes redrawn per account.

| Series | Accounts | Cold: accounts/s | Warm: accounts/s | Root iterations: cold | warm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 200 | 9195 | 23408 | 31.11 | 6.22 |
| 30 | 200 | 2170 | 3266 | 81.33 | 20.76 |
| 100 | 200 | 36.56 | 56.3 | 383.7 | 85.14 |

Batches use the defaults (`rel_gap` 1%, 200,000 nodes). Warm starts change only the root LP; branch-and-bound nodes start from their own bases either way.
