# Benchmarks

Generated by `build/pairing_bench` (`bench/bench.cpp`) from fixed seeds; see `bench/books.hpp` for the generators. Requirements and counts are deterministic; times depend on the machine. This run: the owner's Mac (M3 Max, Apple clang, on battery), 2026-11-27, for v1.2.0, with `tools/run_guarded.sh -m 6144 -t 3000 -- build/pairing_bench > docs/benchmarks.md` (about 3 minutes).

## Savings against greedy

Each family's books are solved three ways under the default rule set: every position margined on its own, the greedy baseline (`greedy` in `src/greedy.cpp`), and the solver. Books in these families have up to about 40 series, so the solver runs exact (`rel_gap = 0`). Savings are relative to the all-standalone requirement; the excess is greedy's requirement above the solver's, relative to greedy's.

| Family | Books | Series (mean) | Greedy saves | Solver saves | Greedy excess: mean | median | max | Solver strictly better |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| retail | 495 | 6.52 | 56.07% | 58.70% | 5.59% | 0.38% | 54.64% | 250 |
| adjusted | 492 | 6.59 | 37.06% | 48.24% | 16.48% | 9.60% | 79.79% | 332 |
| adversarial | 100 | 8.29 | 48.33% | 68.42% | 39.23% | 37.23% | 63.60% | 100 |
| market_maker | 200 | 25.2 | 32.90% | 40.08% | 10.99% | 10.17% | 35.78% | 198 |

The adversarial family is selected, not constructed: the 100 of 4,000 seeded retail books on which greedy does worst.

## Batch throughput

Accounts that hold the same series in different sizes share the model's matrix and costs; only bounds differ (spec §3.2), so one account's root basis can start the next. Each batch is 200 accounts on one chain: the same series and signs, sizes redrawn per account.

| Series | Accounts | Cold: accounts/s | Warm: accounts/s | Root iterations: cold | warm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 200 | 27411 | 30406 | 23.44 | 5.435 |
| 30 | 200 | 8468 | 9308 | 41.21 | 19.82 |
| 100 | 200 | 976.6 | 886.7 | 158.1 | 85.06 |

Batches use the defaults (`rel_gap` 1%, 20,000 nodes). Warm starts change only the root LP; branch-and-bound nodes start from their own bases either way.

## Pre-trade check

A resize adds or removes 1 to 10 contracts of a held series without closing it; a new series is a series the book does not hold; a close sells or buys back a whole position. Against cold is the order's total less a cold `solve()` of the book after the order, relative to it, over the first 20 orders of each kind (10 at 5,000 series).
